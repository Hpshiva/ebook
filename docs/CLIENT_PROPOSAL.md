## Summary

You sell digital products (PDF guides and e-books) and want Instagram micro-creators to promote them. Finding the right creators by hand is slow, and existing tools charge a high monthly fee.

Creator Finder is your own tool for this. You choose a niche and it gives you a list of creators who match your four rules, with their public email, a quality score and a ready-to-edit outreach message. You pay once for the tool. Running costs start at ₹0 and grow only with how much you use it.

A working version already exists. It has been tested on real Instagram profiles and can be demonstrated today.

## The problem

- Searching Instagram by hand for creators who fit all four rules takes hours.
- Subscription tools are expensive and charged every month, used or not.
- There is no simple place to track who was contacted, who replied and who became a partner.

## The solution

One web application that:

1. **Finds creators** in any niche and country.
2. **Checks each one** against your rules:
   - 10,000 to 100,000 followers
   - 50,000 to 200,000 median reel views
   - not already selling their own products
   - public email on the profile
3. **Explains every decision**, so you can see why a creator was accepted, rejected or needs a look.
4. **Scores each lead** out of 100.
5. **Tracks the relationship** from first contact to partnership and commission.
6. **Exports** clean Excel and CSV lists.

All four ranges can be changed at any time.

## Main features

- Dashboard with live numbers
- Creator search with filters, plus import of your own username lists
- Creator database with search, tags, notes, owners and follow-up dates
- "Needs review" queue for unclear cases, with one-click approve or reject
- Similar-creator suggestions
- Campaigns, including separate client projects with their own login
- Outreach drafts from editable templates (you review and send from your own email)
- Partnership tracking: commission %, promo codes, revenue, amount owed
- Excel and CSV exports
- Cost and usage page with a monthly budget that cannot be exceeded silently

## Benefits

- **One-time cost** for the tool instead of a monthly subscription.
- **Low running cost.** Starts at ₹0. In a real test search, checking 30 creators cost ₹17.64 in total, about ₹0.59 per creator.
- **Yours to keep.** Your data, your lists, your rules.
- **Two ways to earn:** your own partnerships, and creator research as a service for other businesses.

## What it cannot promise

- It does not find every Instagram account. It searches recent reels under hashtags you choose, or checks a list of usernames you give it.
- **Your four rules together are strict.** In one real test search of 30 fitness creators, none passed all four and 1 was worth a manual look. Only 1 of the 8 creators with 10K–100K followers had typical reel views of 50K or more, and most creators do not write an email in their bio. Lowering the minimum reel views is the change most likely to produce more leads; the tool lets you adjust it at any time. Creators without an email are therefore kept in a "Needs review" list so you can contact them by Instagram message; you can switch this off to reject them instead.
- "Not selling" is a careful rule-based check, not a guarantee. A quick human look at the final list is part of the process.
- Reel views come from each creator's recent posts, not their whole history.
- A high lead score does not guarantee sales.
- Live data collection uses a third-party service (Apify). Instagram's terms restrict automated collection, so this carries some platform risk that you accept as the business owner.

## Acceptance criteria

The product is accepted when, on your account:

1. A hashtag search in a niche of your choice returns real creators with a clear accepted / needs review / rejected result and the reason.
2. Each of the four rules can be changed and the results change accordingly.
3. Results can be exported to Excel and CSV.
4. A creator can be moved through the pipeline, added to a campaign and given an outreach draft.
5. The cost page shows usage, and a search that would exceed the budget is blocked.

## Assumptions

- You provide your own accounts for the data provider and hosting, and pay those providers directly.
- The data provider continues to offer public profile data in a similar form and price.
- You are responsible for how collected contacts are used, including outreach and data-protection rules in the creators' countries.

## Exclusions

- Automatic sending of emails or Instagram messages
- Guaranteed number of leads or guaranteed sales
- Data from private profiles
- Provider and hosting fees
- Legal advice
